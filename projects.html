2025–2026

Current competition cycle and active technical development.

Our main team effort for the year, bringing together design, testing, outreach, business strategy, and real-world engineering.

Development of our core device, including structural design, energy capture concepts, and subsystem integration.

Prototype Testing

Bench testing, iteration, and validation to improve performance, durability, and reliability.

Sensor integration, power generation, and data collection systems that support our converter design.

Materials Research

Investigating material choices that improve strength, corrosion resistance, and long-term performance in marine environments.

Outreach and Education

Public-facing efforts that promote marine energy, team growth, and broader engagement with the Blue Economy.

Early Device Concepts

Initial brainstorming, concept selection, and exploration of possible wave energy converter designs.

Subsystem Research

Early investigation into structures, power systems, materials, and controls that could support a full device.

Team Formation

Building the structure of Washington Wave through recruitment, leadership development, and subteam organization.

2023–2024

Club beginnings, early planning, and vision-setting.

Club Launch

Establishing the vision of Washington Wave and creating a space for students interested in marine energy and engineering.

Mission Development

Defining team goals around renewable energy, education, and interdisciplinary collaboration.

Future Planning

Laying the groundwork for future competition efforts, technical projects, and long-term team growth.
