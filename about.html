About Us

Washington Wave is a UW team dedicated to the development of responsible renewable energy under the umbrella of the Blue Economy. Our team connects a wide range of disciplines to promote education and professional growth within the marine energy sector.

This year, we are preparing to compete in the Marine Energy Collegiate Competition sponsored by the Department of Energy. Our work spans engineering design, testing, outreach, and real world deployment.
